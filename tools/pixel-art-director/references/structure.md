# Construct and review connected forms

Use before constructing animation keys, transforming body/prop parts, or repairing
a proportion, grip, joint or attachment. A cosmetic edit that preserves these
relationships inherits its existing structural evidence; it needs no new blockout.
This procedure owns the structural checkpoint. [Animation](animation.md) owns
phase/timing/material continuity; [comparison review](comparison-review.md) owns
source binding, scope and regression evidence. Use the record at the end here.

## 1. Establish the object before posing it

Open the canonical character/prop reference and actual native baseline. Treat
generated action sheets as pose suggestions until their forms agree with that
reference. Freeze a small reference sheet on a common native grid containing:

- Character: visible head width/height, shoulder, pelvis, knees and soles, with
  approximate upper/lower limb lengths where visible. Mark the actual points on
  an export and record coordinates, uncertainty and reference role. Layer bounds,
  outer silhouette height and ground alignment are not anatomical measurements.
- Prop: body silhouette, mouth/rim or rigid surfaces, distinctive proportions,
  strap roots or other attachment sites, and the contact with hand/body. Give
  persistent names to those sites across poses. Record the chosen front/back
  relationship and which spans disappear behind fingers, rim or cloth.
- Native ownership: inspect visible pixels with relevant owners isolated and
  hidden. Record mixed owners and overlapping old/new marks. A layer's name is
  not evidence that it contains only that body part or material.

Before construction, enumerate the required structural relationships and affected
frames/transitions in the run record from the requested change and its connected
context. Each later decision must cover that inventory. For an irrelevant category
write `none` with the reason; unresolved relationships cannot be omitted as irrelevant.

Use only landmarks relevant to the task. For a hidden site, draw its inferred route
on a separate guide and label it inferred; keep that guide out of the export. A
missing source view permits a clearly named construction hypothesis, not a claim
that the reference proves it. Resolve ambiguity affecting the requested result with
a saved marked guide and simple exported key, inspected beside the reference with
an observation supporting or rejecting the proposed route. Prose alone is not the
study. Stop if the essential route remains uncertain. Do not invent a second prop
design to fit a pose.

**Complete when:** reference and candidate landmarks/sites can be located in the
actual images, ownership is known, and the unresolved structural questions are named.

## 2. Solve structural keys before detailing or propagation

Build a simple pose study for the difficult keys using silhouettes, joint/attachment
guides and a few planes. For an existing detailed asset, retain it and make a
separate simplified diagnostic view; do not destructively flatten the master.
For new animation include rest, the most changed pose and the difficult transition.
A local repair begins with the affected key plus its preceding/following context.

Construct **the connected unit**. For a held bag this includes cuff/hand, handle
paths, roots, rim and adjacent fabric. Decide their angle and overlap together.
Reuse a whole unchanged form where valid; redraw changed contours/planes when
turning or deformation requires it. Independent family scaling, a pasted neutral
head or stretched rows alone do not establish anatomical correspondence.

When replacing a relationship, locate and remove or reshape its obsolete pixels
on their actual owners. Preserve unrelated art. Extend the editable region within
authorized scope to include the seam and its material backing; if that would cross
an explicit user lock, record the conflict instead of painting a second connection
over it. Layers may separate front/back straps, hands and fabric. Choose ordering
from the intended occlusion, not a rule that all handles go above or below the bag.

**Complete when:** the untextured/simplified key depicts the intended anatomy and
object, with a traceable grip→strap→root→fabric relationship. A reviewer must be
able to identify what is attached to what and what covers what. Merely touching
opaque pixels or a line terminating at the silhouette is insufficient.

## 3. Review the real drawing in three complementary views

Use actual native-derived images, not a reconstruction that silently cleans the
candidate. Hash-bind these views using the existing comparison packet's `evidence`
entries and bind its manual review as usual. Keep clean exports beside guides.

1. **Whole form:** compare canonical/rest and candidate at native size and matching
   integer zoom. Check head/body and torso/leg proportions using the marked points,
   then inspect the clean composite. Compare corresponding forms, not independently
   resized bounding boxes. Record measured differences and the pose explanation;
   a fixed head-to-foot height can hide a lower pelvis and shorter legs.
2. **Ownership and attachment:** show the composite, relevant owners isolated, and
   the composite with each relevant owner hidden in turn. Trace each named visible
   root into its material surface; show disappearance/reappearance at occlusions.
   Inspect both ends of each strap, not just the fingers. Look for old branches,
   doubled contours, unsupported protrusions and inconsistent angles. An isolated
   handle can legitimately look detached; judge its intended overlap in the composite.
3. **Neighbors:** show preceding/current/following poses on one grid, plus a labeled
   onion-skin or stepped overlay when useful. Follow the same roots, grip, rim and
   body landmarks across them. If a hand releases/regrips or a root becomes hidden,
   identify the transition. An overlay is diagnostic, never a replacement for clean
   poses. Inspect rest-to-action and return-to-rest as well for a whole sequence.

Projected width, silhouette area and visible limb length may change under rotation,
foreshortening, bending or cloth deformation. Require a visually supported change
of form/overlap; do not enforce constant screen-space boxes or pixel counts. Any
numeric tolerances must be task-specific and established from the reference/pose
plan before evaluating the candidate. They are alerts, not an artistic score.

**Complete when:** each view has been opened, every required relationship has a
concrete observation and each unexplained difference has a revise/uncertain status.
Producing sheets, repeating intended behavior, or listing layer names does not pass.

## 4. Decide before extending the sequence

The agent records `continue`, `revise` or `stop` for proportions, attachment and
neighbor continuity separately. Any `uncertain` required relationship means revise
or stop, even if another dimension improved. At the task's existing independent
review boundary, give the reviewer clean evidence and the structural questions
before producer conclusions. It must identify the actual root/occlusion route and
seek a contradiction. Repeated reviewer agreement is not a substitute for evidence.

Continue autonomously within the authorized correction budget when a concrete
repair is available. If the same structural failure survives a correction without
new evidence, change the construction method or stop that branch; adding another
overlay or another favorable review is not progress. Preserve the best eligible
checkpoint or record none. Do not spend the remaining budget expanding a failed key.

After keys pass, make in-betweens from those established forms and trajectories;
keep the same grip until an intentional release. Frame-by-frame construction means
consistent structure across drawings, not equal tiny displacements or independent
redesigns. Spacing depends on timing. Review **every exported affected frame** and
its joins, including between approved keys; key clearance is not cycle clearance.
Then inspect authored-speed playback when perceptually available, with the existing
honest still-only/runtime limits. A static attachment defect must be resolved in
static review, regardless of playback capability.

**Delivery gate:** an animation may be offered as an improved playback candidate
only after the applicable structural observations support continuation. If they
do not, report the failed experiment and exact unresolved relationship; diagnostic
images may be shown clearly labeled rejected. Do not label it ready for art approval
or leave an already identifiable structural defect for the user to discover.
The user directs style/intent and gives final artistic approval, not routine seam QA.
This is an agent proof boundary, not an extra user permission gate or a guarantee
that visual judgment will never fail. Mechanical checks remain separate.

## Run record

Copy this compact outline into the experiment's observations; omit irrelevant fields.

```text
Native/export hashes and reference role:
Stage: structural keys / detailed keys / affected cycle
Required frames, neighbors and rest/return context:
Required structural relationships, frozen before construction; omissions + reasons:
Marked anatomy points and observed measurements (reference → candidate):
Named prop sites, surface/occlusion route and intended grip/release:
Actual owners; obsolete marks replaced; inferred/hidden sites:
Viewed clean/guide/owner-hide/neighbor evidence paths, scales and packet hash:
Proportions: observed difference + supported pose explanation; continue/revise/stop
Attachment: both ends + root/surface overlap; continue/revise/stop
Continuity: same forms/sites across frames; continue/revise/stop
Contradiction sought / found; uncertainty and required correction:
Reviewer/editor roles; selected checkpoint or none; next action:
Playback perceived / still-only; user approval separately:
```
