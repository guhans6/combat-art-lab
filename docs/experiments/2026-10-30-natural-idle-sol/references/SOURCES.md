# Downloaded motion references

Inspected 30 October 2026. Pattern study only; no reference pixels were imported into our character.

- **Danilo Mattos, Sora / Simple animated character**: [creator upload and CC0 statement](https://opengameart.org/content/simple-animated-character), [original ZIP](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/sora.zip). Saved `sora.zip`, extracted only `Idle.png`, `IdleLeft.png`, `IdleDown.png`; `sora-page.html` preserves the page. Visually inspected the eight 64x64 side-view cells in `sora-idle-frames.png` and the two front-facing cells in `IdleDown-4x.png`. The page says six idle frames, but the actual Idle.png contains eight 64px cells; no source timing is present in these PNGs. Head/shoulder silhouette shifts vertically while the feet retain placement. Sparse poses can communicate a relaxed stance without uniform horizontal stretching. Timing here is unknown; no timing was invented as creator intent.
- **thekingphoenix, New Character!**: [creator upload and CC0 label](https://opengameart.org/content/new-character), [original right-facing idle GIF](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Idle_right.gif). Saved `kingphoenix-idle-right.gif`. Actual 32x32 GIF has fourteen 100ms frames. Visually inspected every decoded frame in `kingphoenix-frames-6x.png`, plus measured repeated frames: F1–6 identical; F7/F14 arm transitions; F8–13 share the lowered upper-body pose. Legs/feet stay fixed, arm moves before the upper-body settle. This is an armed stance, not identical breathing anatomy; lesson is independent overlap/timing and held poses. The head shifts in this example but our head is explicitly locked, so this is not copied.
- **Doodle / Joe Dunkerley, Urban Character Pack**: [creator upload, author and CC0 label](https://opengameart.org/content/urban-character-pack), [original small transparent sheet](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Urban%20Character%20Pack%20small%20transparent_0.png). Saved `urban-small.png` and `urban-page.html`. Visually inspected actual 1000x2040 sheet; street-clothing silhouettes are useful context, but rows/columns have no included timing labels, so no inferred idle timing or strong motion claim is based on it.

Excluded: AdamGDA character template. The creator page explicitly says “No AI/crypto allowed”; did not download, ingest asset frames, or use it as a motion source. [Page](https://adamgamer1111.itch.io/2d-pixel-platformer-character-template).
