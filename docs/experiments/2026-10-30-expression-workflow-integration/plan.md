## Reviewer behavior and finite escalation

The drawing agent receives a bounded task, exact inputs, locks and budget. The final critic opens the authoritative original and exact actual exports, with original/baseline/best/new and genuine 1x plus useful integer zoom. Registration preserves aspect and uses landmarks; matching panel dimensions alone is insufficient. Different source poses or scales make the transform approximate: retain source authority, label uncertainty and inspect the unnormalized original too. Review local curve/placement/relative value, head expression and full-asset reading; a dramatic zoom improvement cannot waive failure at the intended size.

The critic may suggest corrections and may execute a small rescue only within a declared mask/layer/frame scope, recorded ownership and remaining pass budget. Save it as a separate checkpoint and record critic-as-editor, operations and exact export/hash. An edited output needs fresh independent inspection; the editor's earlier review cannot approve its new result. Reuse the task's existing independent reviewer for technical inspection where suitable, and keep user visual selection explicit. An untouched independent Sol visual review is distinct from a Sol-edited candidate pending fresh independent/human visual review. Respect the existing maximum of one automatic Sol/high subagent per user task; do not create an infinite reviewer chain. Ambiguity or an exhausted evidence budget returns the best eligible candidate with a concrete unresolved defect.

Portable guidance names roles (drawer, critic, editor, independent inspector) and failure-driven escalation. Exact model choices belong to the receiving project's instructions or user selection. Model-comparison conditions retain equal assistance and recorded confounds.
