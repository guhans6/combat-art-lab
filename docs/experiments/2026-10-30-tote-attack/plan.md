## Motion and appearance constraints

Primary hip/thorax action drives shoulders→elbows→wrists→continuous handle grip.
At recovery the body brakes before the flexible bag fully settles; arms absorb load,
strap tension is a continuous link and the bag follows a small arc toward the hip.
Use source forms and redraw affected clusters/contours rather than broad row-band
warping. Maintain identity, anatomical limb lengths, bag dimensions, material roles
and a clear intentional two-handed grip until transferring bag to shoulder.
Explicit foot contact and step phases govern the lunge/recovery: do not assert all
feet fixed or force every lowest pixel onto the ground. Toe pivots are intentional.
Face rendering remains recognizable; no relighting, outfit redesign or independent
per-frame palette. Binary alpha and reviewed holes/handle openings need visual review.
