## LuizMelo, Martial Hero

[Artist page and CC0 declaration](https://luizmelo.itch.io/martial-hero); [actual idle GIF](https://img.itch.zone/aW1hZ2UvNjU4NzA0LzM1NDUxNDYuZ2lm/original/aHoV98.gif). `hero-2.gif`; `hero-2-contact.png`. Side-facing fighter, 720×720 preview, eight frames × 100 ms, matching the artist's eight-frame idle listing. Topmost pixel y=120, 128, 136, 136, 144, 136, 128, 120; lowest y=535 in every frame; crop x=260..479, y=520..535 is identical across frames. The hat/head and shoulders lower and rise while scarf flutters and feet remain fixed. Flowing wide garments conceal knees and pelvis, so use for coherent upper-body movement over anchored feet, not proof of articulated knees. Reference looks dynamic/stylized rather than understated.

Practical inference: establish fixed sole contacts and a coordinated modest head/shoulder/pelvis change with leg shapes accommodating it; treat chest detail as secondary and verify the resulting motion at actual scene scale. These samples demonstrate anchored feet and upper-body movement, but do not establish a universal requirement to animate knees or prove how these artists constructed the poses.
