## What must survive simplification

Common to all four: right-facing adult silhouette; tied dark hair with loose flowing locks behind the shoulder; visible warm face and ear; desaturated indigo cloth; dark, low-key clothing; raised worn-metal shoulder and forearm protection; exposed skin between rolled sleeve and bracer; layered diagonal waist wrap, hanging front cloth, and visible sword hilt/guard; relaxed hand and separately planted shoes. The user's new images have more internal detail and shaped lighting/shadows than Current A. Preserve the large highlight and shadow relationships that make those details legible at 152 px, without requiring every speck to survive or bleaching the dark design. At small scale, prioritize the separate hair silhouette, face profile, shoulder/bracer, sleeve bands, crossed waist, sword, two hanging cloth edges, and feet over fine texture.

- **U01:** Quiet largely unmarked face profile, a visible ear, sparse stubble dots along the jaw, and no clearly separate eye observed in the source face crop. Treat the no-eye reading as this source's visual characteristic, not as a rule for other variants. Hair has a small warm tie and soft dark flowing rear strands. The rounded shoulder top catches a broad light plane over a darker lower plate; indigo sleeve gathers, crossed dark waist bands, two differently long blue hanging panels, an understated sword hilt, and loose dark trouser planes define the rest. The lighting is subdued and fine details easily merge into the near-black background.
- **U03:** More articulated, higher-contrast face than U01: dark eyebrow/eye-area marks, nose/mouth profile and fine stubble around jaw; the exact identity of every tiny dark mark is uncertain, so preserve its visual effect instead of inventing extra facial features. Full rear hair, warm tie, and a broad curved shoulder cap that reaches farther across the body are distinctive. The separate upper-arm metal plate, folds in the dark-blue rolled sleeve, shaped bracer, small circular waist buckle, crossed strap, and diagonally raised segmented sword hilt are visible. Broad cool torso/cloth shadow planes coexist with quiet warm highlights.
- **U04:** Coarser, chunkier pixel clusters and broader warm light/shadow planes; more pronounced dark brow/eye-area stroke and a cleaner, angular face/jaw than U01/U03. The tiny dark eye-area shape is visually present, but its precise expression/eyelid interpretation is uncertain. The tied hair has prominent trailing points, the shoulder has a rounded wide highlight over a darker arm guard, and the sleeve, bracer, cross-wrap, two blue hanging pieces and trouser folds are built from larger blocks. Keep the face and outfit's own angular, brighter treatment; do not flatten it to U01 or U03.
- **U05:** Same face, hair, pose, body, clothes, sword, colors, and pixels as U03 outside the shoulder region. Its cap is shorter/narrower across the body than U03. Preserve this localized difference in the static outputs and keep all other treatment aligned as closely as the common source and construction permit. Do not exaggerate the cap difference solely to make it look larger at the preferred display scale.

## Cropping, masking, and alpha review

The supplied standalone images have dark opaque surroundings that are close in value to the character's dark hair, coat, trouser shadows, and boots. The board has additional text, diagram crops, and a bottom scene. Derive a **reviewed spatial mask per source** with edge detail checked against its original, not a full-image color-key operation. Image 3 is the cleanest complete source for the board figure; image 5 needs its own cap geometry. Original background pixels, board labels, and board scenery must not be retained as a rectangle around the sprite.

Watch the thin free hair strands and intentional openings between them; the hair-to-neck/shoulder connection; the sword pommel/handle/guard and dark space beside the waist; the small far hand beside the long sash; the narrow split between the hanging cloth and legs; the leg gap; both dark shoe soles; and the background directly adjoining them. Preserve source-supported curls and attachments while removing matte fringe. Contrast-view the actual transparent export on light, dark and saturated diagnostic mattes, then in the same actual Godot playground. A background-colored edge or small isolated component is a review cue, not proof it should be removed: some are real hair or material, some are source halos. Conversely, a continuity check alone cannot prove an attachment is semantically correct.

The face must be reviewed at native size, matched 152 px display, and integer zoom alongside its own original: dark eyebrow/eye-area information in U03/U05 and U04 should not be erased just because Current A/U01 are quieter. Register the visible profile, ear, nose, mouth and jaw before judging tiny marks. A high-resolution source can contain more facial detail than the target grid admits; report an uncertain or visibly lost distinction instead of fabricating exact features.
