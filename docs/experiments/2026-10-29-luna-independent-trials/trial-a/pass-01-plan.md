# Pass 01 plan

Pass 01 is the initial candidate, not a correction. Its bounded question is:

> Does an exact nearest-neighbour source reduction, followed by deliberate native
> semantic cleanup of the silhouette, face/hair, material planes, and alpha edges,
> preserve the approved character identity and restrained smile at 128x192?

Acceptance evidence will be split into:

- technical: 128x192 RGBA export, transparent background, native source with named
  layers, saved/reopened export equality, and no hidden guide layer in the export;
- appearance: direct original/candidate sheet at 1x and identical 4x, with concrete
  observations for identity, expression, proportions, silhouette, pixel craft,
  materials, and transparency;
- editability: a localized edit on a duplicate with unaffected layers/protected
  pixels checked;
- approval/runtime: user visual approval and Godot playback are not claimed here.

The first exported candidate must be retained as `passes/pass-01.png` and the
supervisor must review it before any second artifact pass begins.
