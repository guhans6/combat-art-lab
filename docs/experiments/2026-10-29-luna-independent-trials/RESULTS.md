# Independent Luna Max image-to-sprite pilot

## Outcome

Two independent fresh-context subagents were launched with the exact requested
selectors: `gpt-5.6-luna` / `max` (trial-a), and `gpt-6-luna` / `max` (trial-b).
No Terra, Sol, or additional model assisted either contestant. The primary
assistant supervised, inspected exports, reported defects, scheduled editor
access, and assembled the final display without changing contestant artwork.
Provider compute, tokens, pricing and cost were not exposed; Max is the requested
configuration, not a measured compute quantity.

Both received the same [brief](BRIEF.md), source and supporting references,
128x192 target, three artistic-pass ceiling, and 120 editor-call ceiling. Each
chose its own preparation and palette. Their own derived seeds were permitted;
earlier outside conversions, correction scripts and peer outputs were prohibited.
Editor slots were serialized. Waiting/scheduling is not an art-quality measure.
This is one end-to-end workflow pilot per model, not a controlled comparison of
model reasoning given identical converted seeds, and not an intelligence ranking.

My visual assessment: 5.6 gives a crisper but noisier source reduction; 6 gives a
more unified reduction and stronger correction workflow. Neither establishes a
convincing final match to the approved expression or deliberate whole-sprite pixel
craft. Six's clearer final smile does not compensate for its closed/angular eye
read. No production replacement is promoted; user artistic selection is pending.

The supervisor comparison shows each first and final export unchanged at actual
1x and identical 3x nearest-neighbor enlargement. Contestants' own review sheets,
including defective versions, remain retained.

## Defect-only review history

Trial A pass 01: inconsistent white/magenta full-character evidence; diagonal
cheek-like mouth and bright/blocky eye treatment; noisy sampled contours; missing
native reopen and protected edit proof. No coordinates, colors or replacement
algorithm were supplied. Pass 02: reopen/local edit evidence improved and mouth
less diagonal; magenta review still missing regions and contour noise remains.
Supervisor independently verified both review PNGs were opaque, ruling out the
transparent-PNG viewer issue. Contestant elected to stop without another editor
slot, acknowledging those limits.

Trial B pass 01: weak near/far gaze and smile; locked flat seed plus overlay did
not demonstrate useful semantic parts; 26 unclassified transparency warnings;
reference panel and labels clipped. Pass 02: mixed ownership in isolated layers
(clothing in tote, blue waistband in jacket, tote fragment in jeans, denim in
contour layer); angular eyes and jaw-like mouth marks; reference still clipped;
missing reopen/edit proof. Final pass improved separation and framing; smile
visible, eyes still insufficiently faithful. The 26 alpha flags are unchanged
source-supported openings, with raw and reviewed checks retained, not erased.
No additional artistic round was allowed after pass 03.

Protocol clarification only: trial B briefly interpreted the ban on previous
converted seeds as banning its own freshly prepared seed. Supervisor clarified
that the same ban applies to outside/peer work for both models. No outside access
was reported. This was not an art solution or correction hint.

commit, push, publication or final user approval occurred in this trial.

## User visual review and supervisor correction — 29 October 2026

The user prefers Luna 5.6's final open-eye treatment, hair detail and sharper,
more visibly pixelated rendering. Luna 6's final eyes were explicitly rejected
as badly distorted. This is a visual direction preference, not approval of every
pixel of trial A or a production promotion.

The supervisor rechecked all four panels in supervisor-comparison.png against
the exact exported PNGs, including stored hashes, 1x alpha composites and 3x
nearest-neighbor displays. Every panel matches exactly. There is no swapped
model label or older artifact used as final: trial A final is pass-02.png and
trial B final is pass-03.png. Final means last retained trial checkpoint, not
artistically accepted output. supervisor-head-recheck.png enlarges the same
unchanged exported heads for direct visual inspection.

Reassessment: Luna 5.6 has the stronger visual result for the user's desired
direction. Its open eyes and sharper, more articulated hair survive better.
Luna 6's final facial correction visibly turns the gaze into angular, squint-like
marks and loses the intended expression. Its more uniform preparation also
sacrifices hair detail. The earlier statement that Luna 6 performed better at
the workflow described documentation/part ownership/correction handling only;
it should not have been presented as an overall winning result. Expression and
identity failures take precedence over those technical gains.

The preparation comparison is also confounded by independent recipe choice:
5.6 used nearest-neighbor reduction and a large source-derived palette; 6 used
BOX reduction and a much smaller quantized palette. Thus the sharper/softer
hair distinction is not solely a test of model reasoning or facial edits.
Observed final visible RGB counts are 253 for A and 35 for B. First-to-final
export changes are confined to facial regions (A: nine pixels, B: 35 pixels),
so the hair difference was already inherited from each initial preparation;
Luna 6's later edits changed the eyes rather than producing that hair difference.
No artwork or contestant output was changed during this review.
