# Independent image-to-sprite trial

## Goal

Produce a usable, deliberately cleaned static pixel-art sprite of the supplied
character from the high-resolution transparent source. Preserve recognizable
identity, pose, outfit, palette relationships and a readable restrained smile at
native size. Demonstrate editable native structure and a safe local revision.

## Equal inputs

- Primary production source: `docs/experiments/2026-10-29-clean-cutout-conversion/inputs/clean-generated-cutout-v1.png`.
- Supporting identity/expression references: `assets/references/approved-2026-10-26/character-scene.png` and `character-closeup.png`.
- Target: full character on a 128x192 transparent canvas. Keep the same framing policy throughout your trial.
- Follow repository instructions and the pixel-art-director skill, including assisted reconstruction and alpha coverage guidance. Historical v03 profiles and masks are not this task's baseline.

Do not inspect existing converted seeds, previous cleanup or mouth fixes, other
trial outputs, prior operation ledgers, or hard-coded artistic correction scripts.
Required repository context may describe earlier findings; do not follow its
links to prohibited artifacts. Develop your own preparation, part choices, pixel
cleanup and verification from the equal inputs. No new image generation.

## Independence and resources

Work only in your assigned trial directory. You are not alone: preserve every
pre-existing file and do not modify global configuration, existing references or
the shared handoff. No commits, publishing, installations or external services.
No subagents or communication with another contestant or other model.

Available tools and instructions are identical. Use the existing authorized
dedicated Aseprite MCP backend; no UI, Code-MCP, generic Lua enablement or direct
Aseprite CLI fallback. Local deterministic preparation and verification scripts
are allowed. Do not call Aseprite until the supervisor grants your editor slot;
after the grant, use only your own documents and explicit identifiers, never
close or modify another document. You may inspect inputs and prepare local code
before the grant. No editor-mode switches.

Budget per trial: three artifact passes total (initial plus at most two
corrections), and 120 Aseprite/editor calls total across those passes. Keep all
attempts including failures. Count actual calls when observable; distinguish
unknown costs from zero. A failed technical attempt consumes the corresponding
call budget, and any exported candidate consumes a pass. If a real tool blocker
occurs, report it precisely and continue independent preparation.

## Workflow

1. Read required instructions, visually inspect input originals, freeze input
   hashes and a concise visual brief. Choose your own deterministic conversion,
   tracing or hybrid method, palette policy and useful semantic native parts.
2. Prepare and construct an editable Aseprite sprite, clean it deliberately,
   export it, and inspect actual exports against the originals at 1x and 4x.
3. Save pass-01 native source, PNG, source/candidate review sheet with native-size
   and nearest-neighbor enlarged views, and technical observations. Send paths
   and wait for supervisor feedback before advancing to another artifact pass.
4. Supervisor feedback identifies observed mistakes only. Decide how to correct
   them yourself. Repeat within the fixed budget, retaining every checkpoint.
5. Prove fresh native reopen/export equality and a useful localized edit on a
   duplicate, with protection checks. A proof-only duplicate is not an additional
   artistic pass, but all its editor calls count. Do not overwrite the candidate.

Deliver exact input hashes, recipe, preparation/drawing operations, native
sources, transparent exports, retained attempts, comparisons, raw check results,
reproduction instructions and honest limits. Separate technical validity,
appearance, user approval and runtime claims. No animation or Godot is required.

## Supervisor review rubric

Identity/expression; proportions/pose; silhouette and transparency integrity;
pixel craft and material readability; useful native editability; reproducibility
and preservation. Compare at actual 1x and identical 4x display sizes. A passing
file or greater changed-pixel count does not establish better art.

This is one independent pilot per exact model at requested Max effort, not a
statistical intelligence benchmark. Any access, tooling or instruction differences
must be reported. Record requested configuration separately from unobserved
provider compute or cost.
