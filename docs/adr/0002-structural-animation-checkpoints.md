# ADR 0002: establish connected forms before cycle expansion

- Date: 31 October 2026
- Status: accepted for the local workflow at the user's request; effectiveness on a
  new art task remains unproven.

## Context

Repeated tote attempts preserved wrong body proportions and introduced handles
that touched the bag without depicting a convincing attachment. Source scale,
foot registration, editable layers, comparison crops and native/export equality
all passed useful but weaker checks. Existing visual instructions and repeated AI
reviews failed to reject the result. The [cause audit](../experiments/2026-10-31-attack-cause-audit/record.md)
records the source and exact current F9 evidence.

## Decision

Adopt the director's [connected-form procedure](../../tools/pixel-art-director/references/structure.md)
as the structural proof boundary before detailed keys and sequence expansion.
Reference anatomy and persistent object sites guide the construction. Actual owner
isolation/hide views and adjacent poses expose incompatible overlays and scale drift.
Repairs encompass the affected joint and material surface, within existing authority.
Explicit structural uncertainty blocks an improved-result claim and propagation.

Keep native layers, existing comparison/protection checks, authored-speed and Godot
boundaries. Do not introduce automatic artistic scores, a new backend, a mandatory
rig, a model-tier claim, or user approval of every intermediate frame.

## Consequences and validation

Agents do a small structural study before expensive full-cycle work and may stop
with an honestly rejected experiment. Valid foreshortening and hidden roots remain
possible; fixed bounding boxes and universal limb/strap tolerances are unsuitable.
Workflow/scenario review can verify the procedure and expose known failures; it
cannot prove future model perception. A new constructed sequence and user review
remain necessary to demonstrate improved outcomes. Local source enablement is
separate from portable publication. No artwork is repaired by this decision.
