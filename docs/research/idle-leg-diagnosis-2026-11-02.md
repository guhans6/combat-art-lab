## Evidence and diagnosis

Viewed all four actual exported poses in `r03-frames.png` at native pixel grid enlarged 4×, and extracted leg owners in `pants-owners.png`. `diffs.png` shows 0→1, 1→2, 2→3: orange removed coverage, cyan added, yellow changed opaque color. In code the 0 pose preserves the REST pants, but 1 and 2 build both pants anew from simple polygons; the entire upper body, arms, pelvis, and coat shift vertically together. The visible trouser silhouette and light planes change while chest, arms and shoulders retain the same relationships. Near-leg left contour goes from x=32 to x=33 at y=105,110,115,120,125,130 on 0→1; both trouser colors/contours change, and boot anchors stay. Composite differences are 97 pixels in y100–117 plus 85 in y118–135, vs 261 throughout y0–81; different ROI sizes and translation mean counts do not establish visual salience. They do establish a broad visible lower-leg redraw rather than simply a moving hidden knee. The rest→moving state swaps construction methods and material shapes, so this discontinuity and the synchronized bilateral forward knee sweep are plausible causes of the exercise reading. Upper body moves, but only as a rigid block; saying it is literally motionless would be inaccurate.
