# Current A: shadows, readability, and real-game references

Investigated **2 November 2026**. The selected size is a **152-pixel rest-body display test**, not a new native sprite resolution. User liked the idle and playground and has reopened the question of how dark the fighter should be. These are observations and a proposed next test, not art approval.

## Local observations

**Yes, the broad shadows and missing small details were intentional.** The corrected Silver Pines concept's `assets/concepts/stealth-fighter/game-specific-r01/silver-pines/corrected-review.md` explicitly says it merged the arm/torso shadows, reduced trouser boundaries, and removed eye marks. The result was a quiet, shadow-led character rather than a promise that every feature should disappear. The user's concern about the *amount* of darkness remains open.

I independently read and composited all four frames of `assets/source/stealth-fighter/idle-r02/idle.aseprite` without opening or saving it. Its SHA-256 is `f4cb8fff0a460cde6845fb15bc0c5e3271259d9d38d5f3efa051e4970b2e6127`; 128×264 canvas, nine visible normal-blend layers, 700/260/500/340 ms. All four RGBA composites exactly match `assets/export/stealth-fighter/idle-r02/frames/idle-00.png` through `idle-03.png`. The rest silhouette occupies 240 source pixels vertically; the [diagnostic](current-a-152-study.png) shows an approximately 152-pixel rest body and an exact 2× nearest-neighbour enlargement on a slate matte. This diagnostic is not a Godot capture.

In the actual existing playground, the character is drawn from the source PNG with nearest filtering and no character tint, CanvasModulate, Light2D, or custom lighting shader. Its earlier recorded render audit found zero opaque-RGB mismatches at native size. The dark appearance in the unlit baseline is therefore primarily in the drawn colors, not a light mistakenly darkening the character.

At 152px in the new Godot study, the face, hand, shoulder armor, silhouette and some indigo fabric remain identifiable. The *inner* edge of the near arm/guard competes with the dark vest around the chest and waist; the hilt/belt and upper indigo cloth become easy to miss against adjacent dark areas; the upper legs read as one large mass, with separation relying more on the outer contour and narrow lower-leg gap than internal trouser planes. The brighter window and floor can recover parts of the outer silhouette even while the interior stays quiet. A lack of pupils or a drawn-on eye is deliberate in this design, not evidence of a missing eye asset. The individual fold/armor details are also often intentionally absent; the usability of the remaining shapes at this scale is a separate artistic judgment.

In the separate 152px test, I inspected the three actual Godot screenshots: unlit sprite colors, 35% ambient dim room, and that dim room with a warm PointLight2D fill. The dimmed view makes the already-dark figure harder to distinguish. The lamp makes the face, hand and shoulder more visible, but the large vest and leg areas remain simple dark shapes. It is a deliberately limited point-light demonstration with no normal maps or occluders, not a validated final lighting pipeline; implementation and runtime-check details belong to the study's parent record.

## Published visual evidence

These are first-party publication images, inspected as stills. Displayed image pixels are not known native sprite pixels. Full provenance, source URLs, hashes and exact crop operations are in [provenance.json](provenance.json). They are reference material only; reuse or publication rights have not been established.

- **Silver Pines — a directly relevant character-and-scene comparison.** Wych Elm and Team17's [3 September 2026 dev blog](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2333000/announcements/) contains [this original GIF](https://clan.fastly.steamstatic.com/images/45060161/e120a733f7f7dc8f7cd87bd75dd17dd0b474dcfb.gif). Its [near-matching seated poses](silver-pines-45-114-183.png) show the actual dim in-game scene, drawn character, and filmed performance, respectively. The drawn coat has a broad mid-brown plane and very dark trousers; in the shown scene the coat is subdued and the pale face plus seated posture stand out. Some arm and leg detail is also genuinely hard to distinguish in this low-key shot. This is strong evidence that source painting and scene presentation can contribute differently; it is **not** proof of its lighting or shader implementation, or of readability in every location. The dev-blog media is pre-release.
- **Blasphemous — combat shape plus a strong effect.** The Game Kitchen/Team17's [official game listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/774361/Blasphemous/) supplies [this action screenshot](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/774361/ss_bd57bcb1e9183cbea61339727a97bcc5206677b2.1920x1080.jpg?t=1780479368); [1:1 contextual crop](blasphemous-action-crop-1x.png). In this particular dark-brown scene, lighter armor and a tall helmet mark out the fighter, while a pale curved slash makes the strike direction obvious; the slash also covers parts of both figures. This is a useful combat contrast example, not proof that an attack's anticipation or timing reads without effects.
- **Hyper Light Drifter — dark staging with a brighter focal character.** Heart Machine's [official game listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/257850/Hyper_Light_Drifter/) supplies [this campfire scene](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/257850/ss_e786b1d9c66eb9c61e4da2945fe1d9faeb2af736.1920x1080.jpg?t=1762899660); [1:1 contextual crop](hyper-light-drifter-campfire-crop-1x.png). Much of the world is near-black or deep violet, while a red/pink character mass and the light-colored fire draw the eye. It is a useful *contrast* to Current A's subtler palette, not a request to copy those saturated colors or a comparable human anatomy reference.

## Published pipeline statements

Separately from what the images show, the Silver Pines developers [describe](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?enddate=1788457994&feed=steam_community_announcements) filming and drawing over performances frame by frame, using shadows to suggest volume, and rendering 2D sprites in a 3D simulation. Heart Machine's [game listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/257850/Hyper_Light_Drifter/) says its characters and background elements are hand-animated. Neither statement establishes a technique for this project or the shader or lighting settings behind a specific screenshot. These stills were inspected, not watched as real-time animation.

[Godot's 2D lighting documentation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/2d/2d_lights_and_shadows.html) explains ambient modulation, 2D lights, and optional normal/specular maps through CanvasTexture. An ordinary light can brighten or darken existing color and create spatial light variation, but it cannot recover the particular cloth seams, planes, or other authored features that were never drawn. A separate normal map can encode surface-direction information for more varied light response; it does not establish the missing original design. Do not make normal-map production the next step on this evidence.

## Inference and one bounded next test

Keep the accepted dark, sparse identity, but test **one** carefully limited value-separation pass in a separate native copy: a small existing-palette midtone plane at the near-arm/vest overlap, and one restrained plane to distinguish the front and rear upper legs where they merge. Examine whether the existing indigo or guard/belt plane needs similarly minimal separation; do not add facial features, noise, decorative seams, a bright outline, or a uniform exposure lift. Preserve the silhouette, hair, beard, face, armor shapes, clothing/color family, weapon, frame count/timing, accepted idle motion, and originals. Apply any value relationships consistently across the existing four frames. Compare exact before/after at the same 152px scene size, against both the unlit baseline and the same bounded lamp setting, and at integer native zoom; retain both versions and invite the user to judge whether the added separation is worth the change.

This would test idle material/part readability only. These sources and stills cannot establish the readability of future attack windups, strike/contact poses, timing, full moveset consistency, or a final dynamic-lighting system. Those need separate posed, effects-off and actual-speed gameplay tests. No character change or user approval was performed in this research.
