# Visual review — two-routes starter

Reviewed `previews/contact-sheet.png` and `evidence/source-versus-seed.png` from the actual six native exports. The primary three-key natives preserve neutral, windup, and swing, so a beginner can work through a compact trace/cleanup exercise without having to perform a six-key animation around blank artwork.

This is usable as a beginner-practice starter: the blank hand-trace art layers provide a safe tracing route, while the automatic draft remains visibly separate with an empty Corrections layer for later cleanup. The six labeled keys remain a useful optional pose study.

It is not suitable as a final game asset. Decoded native figure heights are 92–97 pixels (about 96), with no native clipping. The small face still has little detail; the source guide retains colored-edge artifacts, and nearest sampling plus the shared 31-color palette visibly loses source detail. Foot placement also jitters relative to the source across the keys. The six-key study lacks inbetweens and returns directly from pose 6 to pose 1.

No Godot playback, final-animation claim, or art approval is claimed. This review asks only whether the starter folders are usable for user cleanup; it does not demand a finished attack or AI art cleanup.
