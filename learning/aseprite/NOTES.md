# Teaching notes

- The learner is new to Aseprite and benefits from precise click/menu instructions and compact visual explanations.
- Use their own female character carrying a tote bag as the motivating example, especially the existing six-pose swing sequence.
- Compare character proportions, silhouettes, tote handle, bag trajectory and planted-foot alignment at *native* scale.
- A reduced-resolution image is not equivalent to deliberate pixel art; resizing may destroy details.
- Maintain reference/working-original separation. Avoid claiming a PNG imported into Aseprite has automatically become a meaningfully structured native master.
- Manual practice is voluntary, not a change to the repository's AI-first production contract.
- Do not assume Aseprite is installed, that the provided AI assets are currently in this branch, that a pixel resolution is approved, or that Godot playback has been done.
- After each real exercise, ask what did not match expectations, then add a learning record; don't record unperformed successes.
