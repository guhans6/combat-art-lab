Lesson 05 / about 10–15 minutes

One pose.
Two ways to begin.

Your win: trace a first tote contour by hand, or correct a small color patch on a prepared draft.

Both primary files begin with the same standing pose at its earlier drawing size. Download hand-trace.aseprite or automatic-draft.aseprite, then use Save As before painting.

Why this drawing is 183 pixels high

The canvas is 128 × 192 pixels: that is the whole transparent rectangle. The actual character occupies a 72 × 183 pixel area inside it. The extra canvas space is padding, not a smaller character. This starter reuses the earlier native pixels and their colors exactly: its 255-entry palette has 248 used RGB colors. It is not a fresh conversion, a new palette reduction, or generated shading.

Route A · trace on blank layers

In hand-trace.aseprite, Guide - hide before export is a visible, locked, full-resolution guide at about 30% opacity. Above it are blank Base colors, Shadows, and Lines and details layers.

  1. Stay on frame 1.In the Timeline, select column 1 on Base colors. Use Pencil to trace just the tote’s outer contour. That one contour is enough for today.
  2. Hide the guide.Your own contour should remain. If nothing remains, check that Base colors was selected, then show the guide again.
  3. Use the actual reference colors.Choose from the existing palette, or open reference.png in another Aseprite tab at full opacity and sample there. Return to Base colors before painting; sampling the faded guide can pick partial transparency. You may practise with two existing colors for a small light/dark shape, but do not force the whole guide into a two-shade rule.

Route B · make a reversible color patch

In automatic-draft.aseprite, all twelve original layers remain: eleven drawing layers and the old hidden guide-source-seed (hidden). Leave that old guide hidden. Corrections is the blank top layer: it is the only place for today’s patch. The prepared source is already a crisp comparison reference, so you do not need to improve it.

  1. Stay on frame 1.Select the column-1 cel on Corrections. Sample a visible source color, then change only 2–6 pixels in one small color patch.
  2. Undo and redo once.Confirm that your intended patch disappears and returns.
  3. Hide Corrections.The original source should appear unchanged underneath. For a later source-pixel deletion, duplicate the actual owning source-part layer and hide its original; do not erase through Corrections.

Prepare the original reach image without AI

For mechanical conversion followed by your own corrections, use the reach conversion launcher (instructions and limits). It applies the saved source mask, resizes and positions the original, and keeps sampled source colors. Open its prepared PNG in Aseprite, save a native copy and correct a duplicate layer. This is a separate flat draft; no animation frame has been replaced.
