# Atmospheric direction study r01 — A self-review

## Scope and evidence

- Artifact reviewed: `raw.png`, 1024x1536, unchanged built-in image-generation output.
- Reference use: the Silver Pines crop informed broad shadow/value grouping only; The Last Night frame informed mood, warm edge light, and sparse dark depth only.
- This is one concept-generation result, not a native sprite, animation, Godot preview, or user-approved appearance.

## What reads

- Full head, both feet, a hip sword, empty relaxed hands, and the single tapered forearm guard are all visibly legible.
- The adult silhouette is taller and less compact than the rejected earlier direction; the long half-tied hair, face visibility, indigo/charcoal cloth base, small rust fastening, cool-dark stage, and restrained warm edge light broadly read.
- It remains a single-character study with no labels, UI, additional characters, neon signage, weapons beyond the sheathed sword, or copied reference figure/pose.

## Candid failures / risks for parent review

- The rendering is too polished and granular for the requested large, simple matte pixel masses. Face, hair, cloth, and guard include many small marks and edge fragments, making it closer to a detailed pixel illustration than an economical in-game character treatment.
- Charcoal shoulder pieces read as fairly substantial armor, and the crossed waist construction plus wraps add more tactical strap/buckle language than intended. It does not form a single large cuirass, but the costume has drifted toward tactical styling.
- The stance is very upright and centered, with only a weak visible weight shift; the relaxed-alert side-three-quarter pose is present but not especially expressive.
- The stage is still fairly sparse, but its warm floor reflection and blocky architecture pull attention away from the minimum character-study setting.
- The face is readable and proportionally adult, but is more portrait-modeled than the requested compact restrained pixel feature/stubble cue. Hair also carries more fine filament detail than the desired few quiet masses.

## Decision boundary

Do not treat this as final appearance approval or as a native production source. Parent review should decide whether its adult silhouette and broad atmosphere are enough to retain as direction evidence despite the material/detail drift.

## Simplification edit — `simplified-01.png`

This is a separate single edit of `raw.png`, retained alongside the raw image. It was not a downsample or filter operation.

### What improved

- The hair is visibly consolidated into fewer larger dark masses, and the background walls/floor use broader, quieter blocks.
- The face, tunic, trousers, and forearm guard have somewhat less fine internal breakup. The adult proportions, central relaxed standing pose, loose half-tied hair length, hands, feet, hip sword, and cool/warm lighting remain broadly recognizable.

### Candid remaining failures

- Simplification helped only partially. The face still reads as a modeled pixel-illustration face rather than a 3–4-cluster face, particularly around the brow, nose, lips, jaw, and stubble.
- Hair retains too many small contour notches and highlights for the requested 4–5 calm locks. The shoulder pieces, crossed waist wraps, sword grip, and boots retain tactical/fussy construction detail.
- The broad block treatment is stronger in the environment than on the character. The character therefore has not fully achieved the Silver Pines-like hierarchy of large matte masses with sparse interior information.
- The edit remains closer to a detailed realistic pixel illustration than to a deliberately coarse sprite treatment. It did not collapse into a pixel-filter image or smooth vector rendering, but it also did not clearly reach the requested economical form language.

### Decision boundary

No further generation was run. Parent should compare `raw.png` and `simplified-01.png` and choose whether the partial reduction in noise is worth retaining; neither is self-approved, a native source, animation, or gameplay proof.
