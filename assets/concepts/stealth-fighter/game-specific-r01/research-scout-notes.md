# Character rendering evidence — 1–2 November 2026

Research references only; no generation or repository modifications. No licence for incorporation of the referenced artwork was established. Treat downloaded files as third-party evidence, not production art.

## Blasphemous

The Game Kitchen's [9 June 2017 character process update](https://blasphemous.backerkit.com/hosted_preorders/project_updates?page=7) describes developing an enemy from concept and silhouette, making the standing sprite, testing it against real scenes, then animating. The site's process image and animation currently return HTTP 410; they were not inspected. The [2019 developer article](https://blog.playstation.com/archive/2019/09/09/the-creator-of-brutal-platformer-blasphemous-out-this-week-on-ps4-reveals-its-real-world-inspirations/feed/) explains the protagonist's religiously derived design.

Visually inspected `/private/tmp/game-specific-research/blasphemous-early.jpeg`, a third-party [early preview image](https://imagenes.hobbyconsolas.com/files/image_640_auto/uploads/imagenes/2023/04/25/69025a65702fe.jpeg) appearing in [Hobby Consolas](https://www.hobbyconsolas.com/noticias/blasphemous-nuevo-juego-creadores-last-door-88184). SHA-256 `94d190c7bf7697d4892e92696b6b36e9743f927ba81dd4245df37e0d4322cf69`. Isolated character has broad planted pose, clustered teal armour planes, small bright glints, dark burgundy limbs and red hanging fabric. Face and hair are fully hidden under a distinctive pointed helmet. It is an early third-party preview, not verified released sprite or native artwork; avoid copying iconic form.

## LUNARK

Inspected `/private/tmp/game-specific-research/lunark.png`, [publisher gameplay image](https://a.storyblok.com/f/235455/3840x2160/18b6d5a8b1/screenshots_lunark-lvl01_01.png/m/fit-in/1920x1600/filters:quality(100)) from [WayForward's game page](https://wayforward.com/games/lunark). SHA-256 `e6430df5148cd19b05221a0e8a8605dc64dc1066db35fb8dfe6bacd41a4f167c`. Leo appears small, with dark blocky hair, light face, red torso and blue leg mass in side profile. Facial anatomy, armor, long hair and sword rendering are not visible. Creator Johan Vinet's [rotoscoping article](https://blog.playstation.com/2023/03/15/lunark-getting-retro-with-rotoscoping/) explicitly concerns cutscene production; do not generalize it to the gameplay sprite. It supports a coarse character language but is a weak reference for finer fighter rendering.

## The Drifter

Inspected `/private/tmp/game-specific-research/drifter-secondary.jpg`, downloaded from [Inverse article](https://www.inverse.com/gaming/the-drifter-release-date-steam-next-fest-demo), [direct image](https://imgix.bustle.com/uploads/image/2025/5/28/a2890ef2/the-drifter-header.jpg). SHA-256 `c445711211c76fb50c6c7647552963aea465765c2c0593a250b9bb6314413dba`. The scene shows human figures with dark hair, warm skin, broad light/dark clothing shapes and relatively long limbs; facial detail is small and compressed and lighting influences the result. It is not an isolated sprite or evidence of precise construction. Powerhoof's [official press kit](https://www.powerhoof.com/press/sheet.php?p=The+Drifter) identifies the game's pixel-art animation and provides press imagery, but attempted downloads from that host timed out. Its video broadcasting permission does not establish general reuse rights for production assets.

None of these sources supplies a complete exposed face, long hair, armor and sword blueprint. Silver Pines remains established separately in the repository's own research. Original fighter design and artwork should remain independent from game character designs.
