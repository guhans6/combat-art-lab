## Parent review

1 has the clearest new graphic rule: larger directional/angular cloth shadows, blue-violet selective edge accents and sharply grouped hair/metal planes. It is the strongest candidate for a cleaner, energetic direction. Hair tips became a little more spiky/windswept and the sword/hand overlap is less clear; these should be corrected if selected.

2 adds sculpted small clusters, broken edges and restrained worn armor texture; it feels quieter and more weathered. The armor and hair risk becoming busy. It does not establish a faithful Moonscars technique; its warm skin/color roles remain the original character's.

3 mixes clean cloth shapes with brighter carved metal and clustered hair. It is between1 and2 rather than an entirely new silhouette. Armor highlights are stronger, chest weathering more illustrative than intended, and some edge accents extend beyond the forearm guard. It may need simplification if chosen.

All retain the same recognizable provisional face/profile, half-tied long-hair arrangement, athletic body, pose, light outfit/equipment and indigo/charcoal/worn-steel/rust material roles. Body proportions and source framing look consistent. Face and hair contours, metal/cloth edges and shading are reinterpreted by the rendering pass; no exact-raster identity claim. All remain stylized pixel-art concepts rather than r01's realistic eight-head illustration, but their fine surface density is higher than original B and must still be judged by the user. Do not label uniqueness or style approval achieved on the basis of generation alone.

These are rendering variants with one silhouette, not three independent character designs. A stronger distinctive costume/body-language exploration can be a separate next experiment after the user selects useful rendering qualities.
